# Limitations & Real-vs-Metaphor Boundaries

> **Epistemic honesty is the primary asset of this repository.** Every boundary
> below is stated explicitly so readers, auditors, and automated systems know
> exactly what is mechanically verified, what is editorial curation, and what
> remains unverified or open.

## 1. Mathematical Scope — What This Repository Does NOT Prove

- **Zero open problems are solved by this repository.** Every open conjecture in `data/problems.yaml` (Collatz, Riemann Hypothesis, P vs NP, Hodge, BSD, Yang–Mills, Navier–Stokes, Goldbach, Erdős–Straus, Lonely Runner, Brocard, etc.) remains **OPEN** unless settled in the external mathematical literature with a verified resolving citation (currently: Connes Embedding 2020, Telescope Conjecture 2023, Erdős Unit Distance May 2026 `arXiv:2605.20695`).
- **`lean/` proves structural lemmas, classical foundations, modular reductions, sharpness bounds, finite exhaustions, adversarial refutations, and a $3n-1$ sibling-map comparison — NOT the open conjectures themselves:**
  - Collatz theorems (36 theorems: Terras bijection, affine & intercept laws, Böhm–Sontacchi cycle equation, parity-word cocycle, no finite 2-adic cover, positivity localization $\rho_w \le -1$, negative cycles $-5$/$-17$, the $3n-1$ sibling facts in `thm_collatz_epsilon_invariance_barrier` (same Terras bijection, positive cycles at $5$ and $17$; the inference that sign-blind arguments cannot settle $3n+1$ is a heuristic, not formalized), chiral sign conjugacy $T^d(-x) = -T_-^d(x)$, exact mod-3 chiral asymmetry $T(\text{odd}) \equiv 2, T_-(\text{odd}) \equiv 1 \pmod 3$, universal 3-adic intercept unit law $3 \nmid c(w)$, global 3-adic nonzero cycle exclusion $3 \nmid x$, zero/intercept/length invariants, positive-cycle odd-step / positive-intercept / strict subcriticality $3^o < 2^d$, $d=1$ & $1 \le d \le 7$ positive-cycle exclusion, unconditional 1-, 2-, 3-, and 4-odd-step positive-cycle exclusion for ALL $d \ge 1$, sharp universal affine intercept ceiling $c(w) \le 2^{|w|-o}(3^o - 2^o)$, global Eliahou–Steiner cycle amplitude ceiling $(2^d - 3^o)x \le 2^{d-o}(3^o - 2^o)$, 2-adic cylinder isometry, periodic spine congruence, Odd-Unit-Lock valuation isometry, Fine–Wilf spine valuation ceiling, and catalytic reversible cylinder evaluation) are exact structural theorems — general positive cycles with $o \ge 5, d \ge 9$ and divergent trajectories remain **OPEN**.
  - Erdős–Straus Mordell mod-840 covers 834/840 classes (99.29%) plus multiplicative closure, cyclic symmetry, $2 \le n \le 30$, and the 6 smallest exceptional squares $\{121, 169, 289, 361, 529, 841\}$, explicitly leaving general primes in $\{1, 121, 169, 289, 361, 529\} \pmod{840}$ open.
  - Lonely Runner formalizes all-$n$ sharpness of $\frac{1}{n+1}$ (over $\mathbb{Q}$), symmetry group (scaling, periodicity, time reversal), and constructive proofs through $n \le 3$ (2, 3, and 4 runners for all positive integer speed triples), NOT $n \ge 4$ or the general uniform conjecture. These cases are classical (the 4-runner case: Betke–Wills 1972; Cusick 1973). The conjecture is proved for up to 13 runners in recent preprints (Rosenfeld, `arXiv:2509.14111`, 8 runners; Trakulthongchai, `arXiv:2511.22427`, 9 and 10; Sungkawichai & Trakulthongchai, `arXiv:2604.23906`, 11 to 13), far beyond what is formalized here. `thm_lonely_runner_n3_nondiv4` covers 27 of the 64 speed-triple classes mod 4 with the single time $t = 1/4$, not "7/8" as earlier `v0.5.0` prose stated.
  - Brocard formalizes mod-3/4/10 invariants and exhaustive check through $n \le 20$ ($20!+1 = 2.43 \times 10^{18}$), NOT finiteness.
  - Primes/Twins (Euclid, arbitrary prime gaps, oddness $\ge 3$, twin mod-6), Goldbach ($4 \le n \le 100$ + odd binary obstruction), Perfect/Mersenne (Mersenne exponent primality, Complete Euclid–Euler biconditional characterization of ALL even perfect numbers with `divSum_eq_divSumLin` formally equating `divSum n` to $\sum_{d \mid n} d$ for all $n \in \mathbb{N}$ in `v0.7.0`, Euler's 1750 Mersenne prime divisor congruence $2p \mid (q-1)$, $\{6, 28, 496, 8128\}$, no odd perfect $\le 100$), and Ramsey ($R(3,3) = 6$, $R(3,4) = 9$) are classical foundations and finite exhaustions — Twin Primes, Goldbach, Odd Perfect Numbers, Mersenne Infinitude, and higher Ramsey numbers remain **OPEN**.
- **Audit scope:** The independent statement audit of 2026-11-26 ([audit log](https://github.com/0thernes/0thernes-research-archive/blob/main/docs/reviews/2026-11-26-statement-audit.md)) covered `v0.5.0` (commit `0604df5`, 69 declarations). ## 5. External Program Mirror (Left to Solve) — Third-Party Data Boundaries

- `data/external/` and `docs/external/` mirror **all 26 live programs** (62,790 items across 27 shards at $\le 4\text{ MiB}$ per JSONL file to remain strictly under Build Standard A24's 5 MB cap) from [lefttosolve.com](https://lefttosolve.com/programs) daily. Its "solved here" flags are **agent-submitted claims on a third-party site** disaggregated by `kind` (`open` / `known` / `warmup` / `unformalized`) so solved `known` literature theorems, `warmup` exercises, and unverified agent claims on `open` conjectures (4 crosswalk review signals as of `2026-11-27`) are never misread as verified mathematical solutions; none have been verified by this Atlas. Neither counts nor flags ever modify `data/problems.yaml`; mismatches only appear as review signals in `docs/external/CROSSWALK.md`.
- **Metadata only & CC BY-SA 4.0 licensing carve-out:** Each mirrored JSONL record stores identifiers, titles, kinds, fields, prizes, and `solved` flags only — **zero statement excerpts** and zero volatile counters. Third-party titles (Wikipedia, MathOverflow, Mathematics Stack Exchange, OEIS, etc.) retain their upstream licenses (`CC BY-SA 4.0` / `Apache-2.0` / upstream terms) and `data/external/lefttosolve/` + `docs/external/` are **explicitly carved out of this repository's CC BY 4.0 data license** (see `LICENSE` and `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`). Raw Q&A/comment streams (`MathOverflow`, `Mathematics Stack Exchange`, `OEIS conjectures (not formalized)`) are mirrored in full for offline search (`scripts/search_all_problems.py`) but excluded from automatic title-based crosswalk matching to prevent noisy false positives.
- The crosswalk is a **title/identity-level** correspondence (normalized-title rule + curated overrides), not a statement-fidelity certification; a matched formal statement may be a variant or a stronger/weaker form.
- The daily job depends on an external website and API whose format can change; fail-closed guards abort the sync without writing (`< 99.5%` completeness or `> 20%` shrink), so the committed snapshot can be stale — `last_changed_utc` in `snapshot.json` records the last content change.
- The mirror is excluded from `data/MANIFEST.sha256` and the dual timestamps because it changes daily; integrity is the per-snapshot content SHA-256 plus git history.
