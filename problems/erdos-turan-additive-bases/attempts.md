# Attempts — The Erdős–Turán Conjecture on Additive Bases

_Notable attempts, near-misses, retracted proofs._

- **Over-$\mathbb{Z}$ Counterexample (Nathanson 2003).** Demonstrates decisively that the conjecture is not a general theorem about abelian groups: one can inductively build $A \subseteq \mathbb{Z}$ covering every integer uniquely as an unordered sum $a + b$.
- **Unrefereed Preprint Claims.** Because the statement is elementary, numerous unrefereed manuscripts (including preprint server uploads claiming elementary pigeonhole or generating-function proofs) have appeared over the decades; none has passed peer review, typically overlooking either the $\mathbb{Z}$ vs $\mathbb{N}$ obstruction or subtle cancellation in $\sum r_A(n) z^n$ near the boundary $|z| = 1$.
- **Stronger Density Form ($a_k \ll k^2$).** Even the finite-constant lower bounds proved for full bases remain open for the weaker hypothesis $a_k \ll k^2$ (the Erdős–Sárközy strengthening), where even proving $\limsup r_A(n) \ge 2$ fails (Sidon sets exist with $a_k \ll k^3$, and Ruzsa constructed a Sidon set with $a_k \ll k^{\sqrt{2}+1+\varepsilon}$, leaving the $k^2$ threshold open!).
