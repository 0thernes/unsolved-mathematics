# Top Mathematicians — The Erdős–Turán Conjecture on Additive Bases

_Ten figures who shaped the problem, past and present._

| # | Name | Era | Role | Contribution |
|---|------|-----|------|--------------|
| 1 | Paul Erdős | 1941–1996 | originator | Co-posed the conjecture (1941), proved the $\log n$ upper bound (1956), and settled the multiplicative version (1964). |
| 2 | Pál Turán | 1941–1976 | originator | Co-formulated the conjecture in the 1941 *JLMS* paper. |
| 3 | Wolfgang Fuchs | 1956 | breakthrough | Co-proved the Erdős–Fuchs oscillation theorem on summatory representation counts (1956). |
| 4 | Imre Ruzsa | 1970s–present | breakthrough | Deep work on $B_2[g]$ sets, Sidon bases, and probabilistic additive bases. |
| 5 | Georges Grekos | 2000s–present | breakthrough | Led the Grekos–Haddad–Helou–Pihko finite lower bounds $\limsup r_A(n) \ge 6$ (2003). |
| 6 | Labib Haddad | 2003–2004 | breakthrough | Co-proved the 2003 representation-function lower bounds. |
| 7 | Charles Helou & Jukka Pihko | 2003–2004 | breakthrough | Co-proved the 2003–2004 local analytic and combinatorial bounds. |
| 8 | Melvyn B. Nathanson | 1990s–present | breakthrough | Constructed unique representation bases over $\mathbb{Z}$ (2003), proving positivity in $\mathbb{N}$ is essential. |
| 9 | Peter Borwein, Stephen Choi & Frank Chu | 2006 | frontier | Improved the unconditional lower bound to $\limsup r_A(n) \ge 8$ (2006) via generating-function inequalities. |
| 10 | Javier Cilleruelo | 1990s–2016 | frontier | Sharpest bounds on $B_2[g]$ sets and Sidon set asymptotics governing the local barrier. |
