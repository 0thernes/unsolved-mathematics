# Top Mathematicians — The Cohen–Lenstra Heuristics

_Ten figures who shaped the problem, past and present._

| # | Mathematician | Contribution |
|---|---------------|--------------|
| 1 | Henri Cohen | Co-formulated the Cohen–Lenstra (1984) and Cohen–Martinet (1990) heuristics |
| 2 | Hendrik W. Lenstra Jr. | Co-formulated the $1/|\mathrm{Aut}(G)|$ heuristic law for class groups (1984) |
| 3 | Harold Davenport & Hans Heilbronn | Proved the exact 3-torsion moment for quadratic class groups (1971) |
| 4 | Manjul Bhargava | Pioneered higher composition laws and arithmetic statistics of rings and class groups |
| 5 | Étienne Fouvry & Jürgen Klüners | Proved the 4-rank distribution of quadratic class groups (2007) |
| 6 | Jordan Ellenberg, Akshay Venkatesh & Craig Westerland | Proved the large-$q$ function-field Cohen–Lenstra conjecture via Hurwitz spaces (2016) |
| 7 | Melanie Matchett Wood | Developed random-group / moment methods and non-abelian Cohen–Lenstra–Martinet theory |
| 8 | Will Sawin | Co-developed refined moment-uniqueness and root-of-unity heuristics with Wood |
| 9 | Alexander Smith | Proved the $2^\infty$-class-group distribution and $\ell^\infty$-Selmer twist theorems (2022–2025) |
| 10 | Aaron Landesman & Ishan Levy | Sharpened homological stability of Hurwitz spaces toward fixed-$q$ function fields |
