# Top Mathematicians — The Cohen–Lenstra Heuristics

_Ten figures who shaped the problem, past and present._

| # | Name | Era | Role | Contribution |
|---|------|-----|------|--------------|
| 1 | Henri Cohen | 1984–present | originator | Co-formulated the Cohen–Lenstra (1984) and Cohen–Martinet (1990) heuristics. |
| 2 | Hendrik W. Lenstra Jr. | 1984–present | originator | Co-formulated the $1/\lvert\mathrm{Aut}(G)\rvert$ heuristic law for class groups (1984). |
| 3 | Harold Davenport & Hans Heilbronn | 1971 | precursor | Proved the exact 3-torsion moment for quadratic class groups (1971). |
| 4 | Manjul Bhargava | 2005–present | breakthrough | Pioneered higher composition laws and arithmetic statistics of rings and class groups. |
| 5 | Étienne Fouvry & Jürgen Klüners | 2007–2010 | breakthrough | Proved the 4-rank distribution of quadratic class groups (2007). |
| 6 | Jordan Ellenberg, Akshay Venkatesh & Craig Westerland | 2016 | breakthrough | Proved the large-$q$ function-field Cohen–Lenstra conjecture via Hurwitz spaces (2016). |
| 7 | Melanie Matchett Wood | 2015–present | frontier | Developed random-group / moment methods and non-abelian Cohen–Lenstra–Martinet theory. |
| 8 | Will Sawin | 2020–present | frontier | Co-developed refined moment-uniqueness and root-of-unity heuristics with Wood. |
| 9 | Alexander Smith | 2022–present | frontier | Proved the $2^\infty$-class-group distribution and $\ell^\infty$-Selmer twist theorems (2022–2025). |
| 10 | Aaron Landesman & Ishan Levy | 2023–present | frontier | Sharpened homological stability of Hurwitz spaces toward fixed-$q$ function fields. |
