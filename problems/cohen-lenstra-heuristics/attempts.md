# Attempts — The Cohen–Lenstra Heuristics

_Notable attempts, near-misses, retracted proofs._

- **Roots-of-Unity Corrections (Malle 2008; Wood–Wang).** Careful testing of Cohen–Martinet over base fields containing $p$-th roots of unity revealed subtle discrepancies in the naive 1984/1990 weights whenever $\mu_p \subset K$ (the "root-of-unity" obstruction, corrected via non-abelian / Galois-equivariant random matrix models by Wood, Sawin, and Liu). The original quadratic case over $\mathbb{Q}$ for odd $p$ has no $p$-th roots of unity in $\mathbb{Q}$ and remains completely intact.
- **Unconditional Pointwise Bounds on $p$-Torsion.** Even the much weaker pointwise conjecture $|\mathrm{Cl}(K)[p]| \ll_{p,\varepsilon} |D|^\varepsilon$ (the $\ell$-torsion conjecture) remains open for every odd prime $p \ge 3$ (best unconditional bound for $p=3$ is $|D|^{1/3 - \delta}$ via Ellenberg–Venkatesh 2007).
