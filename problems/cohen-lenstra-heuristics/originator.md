# Originator(s) — The Cohen–Lenstra Heuristics

_Biography, background, and the ideas that led here._

**Henri Cohen (born 1947)** and **Hendrik W. Lenstra Jr. (born 1949)** are two of the founding figures of computational and algorithmic algebraic number theory. Observing curious empirical biases in large class-group tables — cyclic groups occurring far more often than non-cyclic ones, and 3-ranks matching Davenport–Heilbronn — they recognized that weighting finite abelian groups inversely by $|\mathrm{Aut}(G)|$ (the natural "groupoid cardinality" measure!) reproduced every observed frequency with uncanny precision.
