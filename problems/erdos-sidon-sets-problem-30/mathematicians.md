# Top Mathematicians — The Erdős–Turán Sidon Set Conjecture (Erdős Problem 30)

_Ten figures who shaped the problem, past and present._

| # | Mathematician | Contribution |
|---|---------------|--------------|
| 1 | Simon Sidon | Originated $B_2$ sequences in harmonic analysis (1932) |
| 2 | James Singer | Proved the projective-plane lower bound $h(N) \ge (1-o(1))\sqrt{N}$ (1938) |
| 3 | Paul Erdős | Co-proved the $N^{1/4}$ upper bound (1941) and set the \$1,000 prize for $O_\varepsilon(N^\varepsilon)$ |
| 4 | Pál Turán | Co-proved the 1941 sliding-window upper bound |
| 5 | Raj Chandra Bose & Sarvadaman Chowla | Extended projective constructions to affine planes and $B_h$ sets (1962) |
| 6 | Bernt Lindström | Proved $h(N) \le \sqrt{N} + N^{1/4} + 1$ (1969) |
| 7 | Imre Ruzsa | Established fundamental bounds on modular and infinite Sidon sets |
| 8 | Javier Cilleruelo | Unified Sidon set theory, generalized Sidon bounds, and simplified the $N^{1/4}$ proof |
| 9 | Kevin O'Bryant | Compiled the definitive Sidon bibliography and co-proved the $0.98183 N^{1/4}$ record |
| 10 | József Balogh, Zoltán Füredi & Souktik Roy | First improvement to Lindström's leading constant in 52 years ($0.998 N^{1/4}$, 2021) |
