# Top Mathematicians — The Erdős–Turán Sidon Set Conjecture (Erdős Problem 30)

_Ten figures who shaped the problem, past and present._

| # | Name | Era | Role | Contribution |
|---|------|-----|------|--------------|
| 1 | Simon Sidon | 1932 | originator | Originated $B_2$ sequences in harmonic analysis (1932). |
| 2 | James Singer | 1938 | breakthrough | Proved the projective-plane lower bound $h(N) \ge (1-o(1))\sqrt{N}$ (1938). |
| 3 | Paul Erdős | 1941–1996 | originator | Co-proved the $N^{1/4}$ upper bound (1941) and set the \$1,000 prize for $O_\varepsilon(N^\varepsilon)$. |
| 4 | Pál Turán | 1941 | originator | Co-proved the 1941 sliding-window upper bound. |
| 5 | Raj Chandra Bose & Sarvadaman Chowla | 1962 | breakthrough | Extended projective constructions to affine planes and $B_h$ sets (1962). |
| 6 | Bernt Lindström | 1969 | breakthrough | Proved $h(N) \le \sqrt{N} + N^{1/4} + 1$ (1969). |
| 7 | Imre Ruzsa | 1990s–present | breakthrough | Established fundamental bounds on modular and infinite Sidon sets. |
| 8 | Javier Cilleruelo | 2000s–2016 | breakthrough | Unified Sidon set theory, generalized Sidon bounds, and simplified the $N^{1/4}$ proof. |
| 9 | Kevin O'Bryant | 2004–present | frontier | Compiled the definitive Sidon bibliography and co-proved the $0.98183\,N^{1/4}$ record. |
| 10 | József Balogh, Zoltán Füredi & Souktik Roy | 2021–present | frontier | First improvement to Lindström's leading constant in 52 years ($0.998\,N^{1/4}$, 2021). |
