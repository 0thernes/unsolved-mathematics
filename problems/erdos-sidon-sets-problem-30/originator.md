# Originator(s) — The Erdős–Turán Sidon Set Conjecture (Erdős Problem 30)

_Biography, background, and the ideas that led here._

**Paul Erdős (1913–1996)** and **Pál Turán (1910–1976)** initiated the systematic extremal theory of $B_2$ sequences in their 1941 paper honoring Simon Sidon's question from harmonic analysis. While Singer's finite-geometry lower bound came from projective planes of order $q$, Erdős and Turán noticed that their elementary sliding-window Cauchy–Schwarz inequality stopped stubbornly at $N^{1/4}$ — raising the deep structural question of whether finite-geometry constructions are essentially optimal for all $N$.
