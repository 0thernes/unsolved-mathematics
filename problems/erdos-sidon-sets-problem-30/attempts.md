# Attempts — The Erdős–Turán Sidon Set Conjecture (Erdős Problem 30)

_Notable attempts, near-misses, retracted proofs._

- **Fourier Positive-Definite Kernel Barrier (Ruzsa; Kolountzakis; O'Bryant).** Careful optimization of linear programming / positive-definite trigonometric kernels proves that pure linear-programming relaxations on the autocorrelation $1_A * 1_{-A}$ cannot improve the exponent $1/4$, explaining why 52 years elapsed between Lindström (1969) and Balogh–Füredi–Roy (2021) just to shave $0.002$ off the leading constant.
- **Exhaustive Exact Computations.** For small $N$, exact maximum Sidon sets have been computed (e.g. $h(N)$ determined exhaustively into the tens of thousands); empirical values hover tightly near $\sqrt{N} + O(1)$ with no sign of $N^{1/4}$ growth, bolstering belief in the conjecture without pointing to a proof.
