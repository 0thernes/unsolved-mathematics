# Top Mathematicians — The Beck–Fiala Conjecture

_Ten figures who shaped the problem, past and present._

| # | Mathematician | Contribution |
|---|---------------|--------------|
| 1 | József Beck | Co-proved the $2t-1$ bound and formulated the $O(\sqrt{t})$ conjecture (1981) |
| 2 | Tibor Fiala | Co-authored the foundational 1981 Beck–Fiala theorem |
| 3 | János Komlós | Formulated the unit-vector generalization implying Beck–Fiala |
| 4 | Joel Spencer | Invented partial coloring / entropy method for discrepancy ("six standard deviations", 1985) |
| 5 | Wojciech Banaszczyk | Proved the Gaussian-measure theorem yielding $O(\sqrt{t \log n})$ (1998) |
| 6 | Jiří Matoušek | Unified combinatorial & geometric discrepancy theory and partial-coloring bounds |
| 7 | Boris Bukh | Achieved the best $n$-independent bound $2t - \log^* t$ (2013/2016) |
| 8 | Nikhil Bansal | Pioneered algorithmic discrepancy theory and SDP/walk methods (2010–2025) |
| 9 | Daniel Dadush & Shashwat Garg | Co-invented the Gram–Schmidt Walk achieving constructive Banaszczyk bounds (2017/2018) |
| 10 | Shachar Lovett & Aleksandar Nikolov | Deep contributions to Lovett–Meka partial coloring and convex-geometry discrepancy |
