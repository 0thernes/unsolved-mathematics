# Top Mathematicians — The Beck–Fiala Conjecture

_Ten figures who shaped the problem, past and present._

| # | Name | Era | Role | Contribution |
|---|------|-----|------|--------------|
| 1 | József Beck | 1981–present | originator | Co-proved the $2t-1$ bound and formulated the $O(\sqrt{t})$ conjecture (1981). |
| 2 | Tibor Fiala | 1981 | originator | Co-authored the foundational 1981 Beck–Fiala theorem. |
| 3 | János Komlós | 1980s–present | originator | Formulated the unit-vector generalization implying Beck–Fiala. |
| 4 | Joel Spencer | 1985–present | breakthrough | Invented partial coloring / entropy method for discrepancy ('six standard deviations', 1985). |
| 5 | Wojciech Banaszczyk | 1998 | breakthrough | Proved the Gaussian-measure theorem yielding $O(\sqrt{t \log n})$ (1998). |
| 6 | Jiří Matoušek | 1990s–2015 | survey | Unified combinatorial & geometric discrepancy theory and partial-coloring bounds. |
| 7 | Boris Bukh | 2013–present | breakthrough | Achieved the best $n$-independent bound $2t - \log^* t$ (2013/2016). |
| 8 | Nikhil Bansal | 2010–present | frontier | Pioneered algorithmic discrepancy theory and SDP/walk methods (2010–2025). |
| 9 | Daniel Dadush & Shashwat Garg | 2017–present | frontier | Co-invented the Gram–Schmidt Walk achieving constructive Banaszczyk bounds (2017/2018). |
| 10 | Shachar Lovett & Aleksandar Nikolov | 2012–present | frontier | Deep contributions to Lovett–Meka partial coloring and convex-geometry discrepancy. |
