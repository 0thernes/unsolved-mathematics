# Originator(s) — The Beck–Fiala Conjecture

_Biography, background, and the ideas that led here._

**József Beck (born 1952)** and **Tibor Fiala** introduced the degree-$t$ discrepancy problem in their 1981 *Discrete Applied Mathematics* paper *"Integer-making" theorems*, motivated by rounding fractional LP solutions to integer values while preserving hypergraph balance. Beck went on to become one of the founders of modern discrepancy theory (co-authoring *Irregularities of Distribution* with W. W. L. Chen).
