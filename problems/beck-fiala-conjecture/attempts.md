# Attempts — The Beck–Fiala Conjecture

_Notable attempts, near-misses, retracted proofs._

- **Subgaussian Limit Barrier.** Any distribution over $\pm 1$-colorings whose marginals are $O(1)$-subgaussian can only guarantee $O(\sqrt{t \log n})$ by union bound unless higher-order negative dependence is forced across all $m$ sets simultaneously — showing that pure Gram–Schmidt-style subgaussian walks cannot by themselves remove the $\sqrt{\log n}$ factor.
- **Matrix Spencer & Komlós Hierarchy.** The Komlós conjecture ($O(1)$ for unit $\ell_2$ columns) implies Beck–Fiala ($O(\sqrt{t})$); proving Komlós even for $O(\log\log n)$ remains open.
