# Novelty Positioning - 2026-07-02

## Plain Verdict

This repository currently has a rare artifact, not a Collatz breakthrough.

The distinctive contribution is methodological: a persistent, adversarial,
multi-session AI-assisted mathematics workspace that preserves both verified
work and failed proof attempts, then routes them through source checks,
instruments, control systems, quarantine, and audit ledgers.

The mathematical contribution is more modest. The accepted Collatz mathematics
in this workspace is best described as rediscovery and reorganization of known
2-adic / parity-word structure, with some possibly useful presentation margins.
It does not move the frontier of the conjecture.

## What Appears Genuinely Rare

- A naturalistic same-day record of AI sessions producing both proof-like
  failures and the instruments that refuted them.
- A shared git workspace where failed claims are preserved, classified, and
  quarantined rather than silently deleted.
- A standing verification protocol that uses primary-source checks,
  re-derivation, runnable scripts, negative controls, and audit registers.
- A candidate evaluation construct, EDAP, for measuring epistemic discipline
  under sustained adversarial prompting.

This may be unusual enough to be a one-of-one artifact in its exact
configuration. That priority claim is conditional and falsifiable: it is
refuted by any prior dated artifact showing the same combination of naturalistic
peer-model proof fabrication, in-situ refutation instruments, shared persistent
workspace, claim quarantine, and cross-instance review on a canonical open
problem.

## What Is Not New

- No proof or disproof of the Collatz Conjecture.
- No new verification frontier comparable to the `2^71` computational record.
- No theorem comparable to Tao's almost-all result.
- No cycle-exclusion result comparable to Hercher's `m <= 91` result and
  associated odd-term floor.
- No AGI or ASI evidence.
- No validated benchmark yet; EDAP is a protocol sketch.

## Research Tier

The repo is frontier-adjacent in process, not frontier-moving in theorem
content.

It is competing with strong contemporary practice in the sense that it uses
high-throughput verification, source grounding, adversarial review, controls,
and reproducible scripts. It is not competing with the best Collatz mathematics
as a theorem advance.

## Safe Public Language

Use:

> This work contributes a rare, artifact-rich case study in AI-assisted
> mathematical verification and failure containment on Collatz. The conjecture
> remains open; the accepted mathematics is largely rediscovery with possible
> presentation margins.

Avoid:

> We solved Collatz.

Avoid:

> This is AGI/ASI evidence.

Avoid:

> The in-repo proof sketches close the positive survivor gap.

Use instead:

> The in-repo experiments identify theorem targets and falsify several tempting
> proof routes. They are finite evidence and research scaffolding, not a proof.

## Operational Recommendation

Keep the repo's public surfaces synchronized around this stance:

- `status.md` should say the conjecture is open and identify the rare asset as
  verification discipline.
- `README.md` should list speculative proof sketches as quarantined or
  theorem-target material, never as accepted closures.
- The whitepaper should present novelty as a methodological/artifact claim,
  not a mathematical breakthrough claim.
- GitHub should publish the artifact trail, but the branch or release notes
  should explicitly state that the artifacts are evidence for process and
  falsification discipline, not proof of Collatz.
