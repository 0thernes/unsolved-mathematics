# Originator(s) — The Happy Ending Problem (Erdős–Szekeres Conjecture)

_Biography, background, and the ideas that led here._

**Esther Klein (1910–2005)** posed the original geometric question to the young Budapest mathematics circle in 1933. **Paul Erdős (1913–1996)** and **George Szekeres (1911–2005)** published the 1935 *Compositio Mathematica* paper that established both finite Ramsey theory in geometry and the famous cups-and-caps decomposition. George Szekeres remained devoted to the problem for over seventy years, co-authoring the computer-assisted proof of $\mathrm{ES}(6) = 17$ at age 94.
