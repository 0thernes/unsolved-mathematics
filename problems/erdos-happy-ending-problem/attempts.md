# Attempts — The Happy Ending Problem (Erdős–Szekeres Conjecture)

_Notable attempts, near-misses, retracted proofs._

- **Direct Oriented-Matroid / Pseudoline Generalization.** The Erdős–Szekeres conjecture can also be asked for abstract order types (arrangements of pseudolines rather than straight lines). Interestingly, Suk's $2^{n + o(n)}$ bound holds even in the pseudoline setting (Holmsen et al. 2020), suggesting the exact $2^{n-2} + 1$ formula may not require Euclidean metric stretchability — though no proof of $2^{n-2}+1$ exists even for stretchable point sets.
- **Computational Attacks on $\mathrm{ES}(7) = 33$.** While $\mathrm{ES}(6) = 17$ fell to Szekeres–Peters (2006) and was later independently re-verified via SAT solvers (Marić 2019), the number of order types explodes super-exponentially between $17$ and $33$, leaving $\mathrm{ES}(7)$ unresolved.
