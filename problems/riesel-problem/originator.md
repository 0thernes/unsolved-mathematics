# Originator(s) — The Riesel Problem

_Biography, background, and the ideas that led here._

**Hans Riesel (1929–2014)** was a pioneer of computational number theory at the Royal Institute of Technology (KTH) in Stockholm. In 1957 he discovered the 18th Mersenne prime $2^{3217}-1$ on the Swedish BESK computer, authored the classic monograph *Prime Numbers and Computer Methods for Factorization*, and developed the Lucas–Lehmer–Riesel (LLR) deterministic primality test specifically for $k \cdot 2^n - 1$.
