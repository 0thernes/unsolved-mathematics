# Originator(s) — Erdős Problem 142 (Asymptotic Density of $k$-AP-Free Sets)

_Biography, background, and the ideas that led here._

**Paul Erdős (1913–1996)** was the most prolific problem-poser of twentieth-century mathematics, publishing more than 1,500 papers with over 500 collaborators across combinatorics, number theory, probability, and discrete geometry. Working alongside Pál Turán in 1936, Erdős introduced $r_k(N)$ to isolate the structural mechanism behind van der Waerden's theorem on monochromatic progressions.

Erdős attached his largest cash prize (**\$10,000**) to the asymptotic determination of $r_k(N)$ (exceeding even his \$5,000 prize for the reciprocal-sum Erdős–Turán conjecture #3), emphasizing that merely knowing $r_k(N)/N \to 0$ hides the true threshold between random-like and algebraically structured subsets of the integers. The formal Lean specification tracked in `google-deepmind/formal-conjectures` (`Erdos142.erdos_142`) encodes this exact \$10,000 open problem.
