# Top Mathematicians — Erdős Problem 142 (Asymptotic Density of $k$-AP-Free Sets)

_Ten figures who shaped the problem, past and present._

| # | Mathematician | Contribution |
|---|---------------|--------------|
| 1 | Paul Erdős | Co-posed the density conjecture (1936) and offered the \$10,000 prize for the asymptotic formula of $r_k(N)$ |
| 2 | Pál Turán | Co-formulated the $r_k(N)$ extremal framework and additive density problem (1936) |
| 3 | Felix Behrend | Constructed the spherical-shell 3-AP-free lower bound $N \exp(-c\sqrt{\log N})$ (1946) |
| 4 | Klaus Roth | Proved the first quantitative upper bound $r_3(N) \ll N / \log\log N$ via Fourier analysis (1953) |
| 5 | Endre Szemerédi | Proved $r_k(N) = o(N)$ for all $k \ge 3$ (1969, 1975), introducing the Regularity Lemma |
| 6 | Timothy Gowers | Invented $U^k$ uniformity norms and proved quantitative bounds for all $k$ (2001) |
| 7 | Ben Green & Terence Tao | Developed nilsequence inverse theory, Bohr-set methods, and the primes-in-AP theorem (2008) |
| 8 | Thomas Bloom & Olof Sisask | Broke the logarithmic barrier $r_3(N) = o(N/\log N)$ (2020) and refined Kelley–Meka to $(\log N)^{1/9}$ (2023) |
| 9 | Zander Kelley & Raghu Meka | Proved the landmark Behrend-type upper bound $r_3(N) \le N \exp(-c(\log N)^{1/12})$ (2023) |
| 10 | James Leng, Ashwin Sah & Mehtaab Sawhney | Achieved the first breakthrough beyond Gowers for $k \ge 5$: $r_k(N) \le N \exp(-(\log\log N)^{c_k})$ (2024) |
