# History — Resolution of Singularities in Positive Characteristic

_Origin, formulation, and timeline._

A **resolution of singularities** of an algebraic variety $X$ over a field $k$ is a proper birational morphism $\pi: \widetilde{X} \to X$ where $\widetilde{X}$ is smooth (nonsingular) over $k$ (and ideally $\pi$ is an isomorphism over the smooth locus of $X$). Over fields of **characteristic zero**, Heisuke Hironaka proved in his Fields-Medal-winning 1964 *Annals of Mathematics* monograph that *every* algebraic variety of any dimension admits a resolution of singularities by a finite sequence of blow-ups along smooth centers.

Over fields of **positive characteristic $p > 0$**, the problem remains one of the deepest unsolved problems in algebraic geometry for dimensions $d \ge 4$. Shreeram Abhyankar resolved surfaces ($d = 2$) in 1956 and threefolds ($d = 3$, char $p > 5$) in 1966; Vincent Cossart and Olivier Piltant completed all threefolds ($d = 3$, arbitrary characteristic $p > 0$, and later excellent arithmetical threefolds) in 2008–2019. Johan de Jong's 1996 *alterations* theorem yields a generically finite (rather than birational) smooth cover in all dimensions, but genuinely **birational** resolution in dimension $d \ge 4$ over characteristic $p > 0$ remains open.

## Timeline

- **1939–1944** — Oscar Zariski resolves algebraic surfaces and threefolds in characteristic zero via valuation theory and local uniformization (`10.2307/1969189`).
- **1956** — Shreeram Abhyankar proves local uniformization and resolution of singularities for surfaces ($d = 2$) in positive characteristic (`10.2307/1970014`).
- **1964** — Heisuke Hironaka proves resolution of singularities in **all dimensions** in characteristic zero (`10.2307/1970486`).
- **1966** — Abhyankar resolves threefolds in characteristic $p > 5$ (extended by Cutkosky 2004).
- **1996** — A. J. de Jong proves that every variety in any characteristic admits a smooth **alteration** (proper, surjective, generically finite — not birational unless degree 1) (`10.1007/bf02698644`).
- **2008–2009** — Vincent Cossart and Olivier Piltant prove resolution of singularities for all algebraic threefolds over arbitrary fields of positive characteristic (`10.1016/j.jalgebra.2008.03.032`, `10.1016/j.jalgebra.2008.11.030`), later extended to excellent arithmetical threefolds (`arXiv:1412.0868`, 2019).
- **2019–2024** — Abramovich, Temkin, and Włodarczyk (and independently McQuillan) achieve rapid functorial embedded resolution in characteristic zero via **weighted blowings up** (`arXiv:1906.07106`, `10.2140/ant.2024.18.1557`), opening new stack-theoretic routes under active investigation in char $p$.
