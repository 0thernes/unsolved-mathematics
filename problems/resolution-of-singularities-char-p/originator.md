# Originator(s) — Resolution of Singularities in Positive Characteristic

_Biography, background, and the ideas that led here._

**Oscar Zariski (1899–1986)** put the Italian school's birational geometry on rigorous commutative-algebraic foundations and introduced local uniformization of valuations. His students **Shreeram Abhyankar (1930–2012)** and **Heisuke Hironaka (born 1931)** defined the modern landscape: Hironaka solved characteristic zero in all dimensions (1964), while Abhyankar exposed the wild characteristic-$p$ phenomena (Artin–Schreier extensions, failure of maximal contact) that still block $d \ge 4$.
