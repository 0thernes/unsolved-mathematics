# Top Mathematicians — Resolution of Singularities in Positive Characteristic

_Ten figures who shaped the problem, past and present._

| # | Name | Era | Role | Contribution |
|---|------|-----|------|--------------|
| 1 | Oscar Zariski | 1939–1944 | precursor | Proved characteristic-zero resolution in dim $\le 3$ and formulated local uniformization. |
| 2 | Shreeram Abhyankar | 1956–1966 | originator | Resolved surfaces in all char $p$ (1956) and threefolds for $p > 5$ (1966). |
| 3 | Heisuke Hironaka | 1964 | originator | Proved characteristic-zero resolution in all dimensions (1964, Fields Medal) and framed the char-$p$ problem. |
| 4 | Aise Johan de Jong | 1996 | breakthrough | Proved existence of smooth alterations in all dimensions and characteristics (1996). |
| 5 | Vincent Cossart & Olivier Piltant | 2008–2019 | breakthrough | Proved resolution for all threefolds in arbitrary positive & mixed characteristic (2008–2019). |
| 6 | Herwig Hauser | 1998–present | survey | Exposed characteristic-$p$ obstructions ('kangaroo' phenomena) and authored definitive surveys. |
| 7 | János Kollár | 2000s–present | survey | Authored *Lectures on Resolution of Singularities* and simplified characteristic-zero proofs. |
| 8 | Orlando Villamayor & Edward Bierstone | 1989–present | breakthrough | Developed constructive/canonical resolution algorithms and characteristic-$p$ invariant theory. |
| 9 | Dan Abramovich & Michael Temkin | 2019–present | frontier | Developed functorial weighted-blowing-up resolution and valuation/alteration techniques. |
| 10 | Jarosław Włodarczyk | 2005–present | frontier | Co-developed weighted-blowing-up resolution and weak factorization. |
