# Top Mathematicians — Resolution of Singularities in Positive Characteristic

_Ten figures who shaped the problem, past and present._

| # | Mathematician | Contribution |
|---|---------------|--------------|
| 1 | Oscar Zariski | Proved characteristic-zero resolution in dim $\le 3$ and formulated local uniformization |
| 2 | Shreeram Abhyankar | Resolved surfaces in all char $p$ (1956) and threefolds for $p > 5$ (1966) |
| 3 | Heisuke Hironaka | Proved characteristic-zero resolution in all dimensions (1964, Fields Medal) |
| 4 | Aise Johan de Jong | Proved existence of smooth alterations in all dimensions and characteristics (1996) |
| 5 | Vincent Cossart & Olivier Piltant | Proved resolution for all threefolds in arbitrary positive & mixed characteristic (2008–2019) |
| 6 | Herwig Hauser | Exposed characteristic-$p$ obstructions ("kangaroo" phenomena) and authored definitive surveys |
| 7 | János Kollár | Authored *Lectures on Resolution of Singularities* and simplified characteristic-zero proofs |
| 8 | Orlando Villamayor & Edward Bierstone | Developed constructive/canonical resolution algorithms and characteristic-$p$ invariant theory |
| 9 | Dan Abramovich & Michael Temkin | Developed functorial weighted-blowing-up resolution and valuation/alteration techniques |
| 10 | Jarosław Włodarczyk | Co-developed weighted-blowing-up resolution and weak factorization |
