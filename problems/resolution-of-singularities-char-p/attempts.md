# Attempts — Resolution of Singularities in Positive Characteristic

_Notable attempts, near-misses, retracted proofs._

- **Hironaka's Characteristic-$p$ Manuscripts (2003–2017).** Hironaka circulated multiple ambitious manuscripts and preprints attempting to resolve singularities in all dimensions in positive characteristic via idealistic exponents and infinitely near singularities; serious gaps were found by reviewers (including Hauser and Kawanoue) and no complete peer-reviewed proof for $d \ge 4$ has been accepted.
- **Kawanoue–Matsuki Idealistic Filtration Program.** Developed a characteristic-free framework aiming to substitute for maximal contact using differential operators of all orders (Hasse–Schmidt derivations); it recovers characteristic zero cleanly, while termination of the invariant in general characteristic $p$ ($d \ge 4$) remains open.
