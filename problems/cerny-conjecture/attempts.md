# Attempts — The Černý Conjecture

_Notable attempts, near-misses, retracted proofs._

- **Pin's Conjecture Refutation (Kari 2001).** Jean-Éric Pin conjectured a stronger rank-compression statement (for any subset $S$ of size $k$, there exists a word of length $\le n - k + 1$ reducing its size? No, Pin's 1978 conjecture on shortening words of rank $n - k$ within $k^2$ steps) which Jarkko Kari disproved in 2001 via an explicit 6-state counterexample, showing that overly local inductive strengthenings fail.
- **Computational Verification.** Exhaustive searches verify the Černý conjecture for all small $n$ (e.g., all 2-letter automata up to $n \le 12$ and general small classes), finding that *almost all* random automata synchronize in $O(\sqrt{n})$ steps (Nicaud 2016) — making the extremal $(n-1)^2$ automata exceedingly rare and rigid.
