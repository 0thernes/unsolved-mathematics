# Originator(s) — The Černý Conjecture

_Biography, background, and the ideas that led here._

**Ján Černý (born 1938)** formulated the conjecture in his 1964 Slovak-language paper *Poznámka k homogénnym experimentom s konečnými automatmi* while studying Moore's homing and synchronizing experiments on finite state machines. Černý's two-letter extremal family — a cyclic shift on $n$ states plus a single merging letter fixing $n-1$ states — remains the unique known infinite family attaining $(n-1)^2$ (and only a handful of sporadic exceptions achieve $(n-1)^2$ for small $n \le 6$).
