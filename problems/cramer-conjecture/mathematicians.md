# Top Mathematicians — Cramér's Conjecture on Prime Gaps

_Ten figures who shaped the problem, past and present._

| # | Name | Era | Role | Contribution |
|---|------|-----|------|--------------|
| 1 | Harald Cramér | 1936 | originator | Introduced the probabilistic prime model (1936) and proved the RH bound $O(\sqrt{p_n}\log p_n)$. |
| 2 | Robert Rankin | 1938 | breakthrough | Proved the classic lower bound for large prime gaps (1938). |
| 3 | Atle Selberg | 1943 | breakthrough | Proved almost-all prime-gap bounds under RH (1943). |
| 4 | Helmut Maier | 1985 | breakthrough | Discovered short-interval prime irregularities challenging naive Cramér heuristics (1985). |
| 5 | Andrew Granville | 1995–present | breakthrough | Predicted $\limsup g_n / (\log p_n)^2 \ge 2e^{-\gamma} \approx 1.1229$ (1995). |
| 6 | Roger Baker, Glyn Harman & János Pintz | 2001 | breakthrough | Proved the unconditional upper bound $p_{n+1} - p_n \ll p_n^{0.525}$ (2001). |
| 7 | Roger Heath-Brown | 1980s–present | breakthrough | Sharp connections between prime gaps, zero density, and pair correlation. |
| 8 | Kevin Ford & Sergei Konyagin | 2014–present | frontier | Co-proved the landmark 2016/2018 breakthrough on arbitrarily large Rankin constants. |
| 9 | Ben Green & Terence Tao | 2014–present | frontier | Co-proved the FGKMT large-gap theorem via hypergraph covering. |
| 10 | James Maynard | 2014–present | frontier | Proved independent and joint breakthroughs on both bounded and large prime gaps. |
