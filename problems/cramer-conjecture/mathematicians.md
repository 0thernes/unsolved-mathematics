# Top Mathematicians — Cramér's Conjecture on Prime Gaps

_Ten figures who shaped the problem, past and present._

| # | Mathematician | Contribution |
|---|---------------|--------------|
| 1 | Harald Cramér | Introduced the probabilistic prime model (1936) and proved the RH bound $O(\sqrt{p_n}\log p_n)$ |
| 2 | Robert Rankin | Proved the classic lower bound for large prime gaps (1938) |
| 3 | Atle Selberg | Proved almost-all prime-gap bounds under RH (1943) |
| 4 | Helmut Maier | Discovered short-interval prime irregularities challenging naive Cramér heuristics (1985) |
| 5 | Andrew Granville | Predicted $\limsup g_n / (\log p_n)^2 \ge 2e^{-\gamma} \approx 1.1229$ (1995) |
| 6 | Roger Baker, Glyn Harman & János Pintz | Proved the unconditional upper bound $p_{n+1} - p_n \ll p_n^{0.525}$ (2001) |
| 7 | Roger Heath-Brown | Sharp connections between prime gaps, zero density, and pair correlation |
| 8 | Kevin Ford & Sergei Konyagin | Co-proved the landmark 2016/2018 breakthrough on arbitrarily large Rankin constants |
| 9 | Ben Green & Terence Tao | Co-proved the FGKMT large-gap theorem via hypergraph covering |
| 10 | James Maynard | Proved independent and joint breakthroughs on both bounded and large prime gaps |
