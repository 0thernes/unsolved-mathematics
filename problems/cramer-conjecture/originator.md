# Originator(s) — Cramér's Conjecture on Prime Gaps

_Biography, background, and the ideas that led here._

**Harald Cramér (1893–1985)** bridged mathematical statistics, actuarial risk theory, and analytic number theory. Earlier in his career he proved sharp conditional bounds on prime gaps from the Riemann Hypothesis; realizing that zeta-zero methods hit an impenetrable $\sqrt{x}$ square-root wall, he introduced the first probabilistic heuristic model of primes in 1936 — a paradigm that reshaped twentieth-century number theory.
