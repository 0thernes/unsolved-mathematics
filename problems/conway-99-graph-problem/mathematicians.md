# Top Mathematicians — Conway's 99-Graph Problem

_Ten figures who shaped the problem, past and present._

| # | Name | Era | Role | Contribution |
|---|------|-----|------|--------------|
| 1 | John Horton Conway | 1970s–2020 | originator | Posed the 99-graph problem and offered a \$1,000 prize for its resolution. |
| 2 | Elwyn Berlekamp, Jacobus van Lint & Johan Seidel | 1973 | breakthrough | Constructed the sister graph $\mathrm{srg}(243, 22, 1, 2)$ from the ternary Golay code (1973). |
| 3 | Andries E. Brouwer | 1980s–present | survey | Leading authority on strongly regular graphs and automorphism restrictions on the 99-graph. |
| 4 | Hendrik Van Maldeghem | 2000s–present | survey | Co-authored *Strongly Regular Graphs* (2022) cataloging all known constraints. |
| 5 | Alexander A. Makhnev | 2000s–present | breakthrough | Proved major restrictions on prime-order automorphisms of $\mathrm{srg}(99, 14, 1, 2)$. |
| 6 | I. M. Minakova | 2000s | breakthrough | Co-proved elimination of large automorphism groups for $\lambda=1, \mu=2$ graphs. |
| 7 | Majid Behbahani & Clement Lam | 2011 | computational | Computationally eliminated numerous candidate automorphism groups of small order. |
| 8 | Willem H. Haemers | 1990s–present | breakthrough | Spectral and interlacing methods for strongly regular graphs. |
| 9 | Sa'ar Zehavi & Ivo Fagundes David de Olivera | 2017 | frontier | Analyzed structural and computational obstructions (`arXiv:1707.08047`). |
| 10 | Joseph Natal & Oleksiy Al-saadi | 2026 | frontier | Recent 2026 structural analysis of spanning subgraphs (`arXiv:2610.16266`). |
