# Top Mathematicians — Conway's 99-Graph Problem

_Ten figures who shaped the problem, past and present._

| # | Mathematician | Contribution |
|---|---------------|--------------|
| 1 | John Horton Conway | Posed the 99-graph problem and offered a \$1,000 prize for its resolution |
| 2 | Elwyn Berlekamp, Jacobus van Lint & Johan Seidel | Constructed the sister graph $\mathrm{srg}(243, 22, 1, 2)$ from the ternary Golay code (1973) |
| 3 | Andries E. Brouwer | Leading authority on strongly regular graphs and automorphism restrictions on the 99-graph |
| 4 | Hendrik Van Maldeghem | Co-authored *Strongly Regular Graphs* (2022) cataloging all known constraints |
| 5 | Alexander A. Makhnev | Proved major restrictions on prime-order automorphisms of $\mathrm{srg}(99, 14, 1, 2)$ |
| 6 | I. M. Minakova | Co-proved elimination of large automorphism groups for $\lambda=1, \mu=2$ graphs |
| 7 | Majid Behbahani & Clement Lam | Computationally eliminated numerous candidate automorphism groups of small order |
| 8 | Willem H. Haemers | Spectral and interlacing methods for strongly regular graphs |
| 9 | Sa'ar Zehavi & Ivo Fagundes David de Olivera | Analyzed structural and computational obstructions (`arXiv:1707.08047`) |
| 10 | Joseph Natal & Oleksiy Al-saadi | Recent 2026 structural analysis of spanning subgraphs (`arXiv:2610.16266`) |
