# Attempts — Conway's 99-Graph Problem

_Notable attempts, near-misses, retracted proofs._

- **Symmetry-Guided Searches.** Exhaustive computer searches assuming even modest automorphism groups (orders divisible by $11, 7, 5$, or large $2, 3$-groups) have returned **zero** solutions — ruling out the symmetric constructions that produced Paley(9) and Berlekamp–van Lint–Seidel(243).
- **Local-to-Global SAT & Heuristic Searches.** Partial constructions pack over 60–80 vertices obeying the $\lambda=1, \mu=2$ constraints before hitting combinatorial dead ends, leaving open whether an asymmetric needle hides in the haystack or a global parity/SDP obstruction kills it.
