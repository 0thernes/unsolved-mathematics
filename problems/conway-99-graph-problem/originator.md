# Originator(s) — Conway's 99-Graph Problem

_Biography, background, and the ideas that led here._

**John Horton Conway (1937–2020)** was one of the most original and wide-ranging mathematicians of the late twentieth century, famed for discovering the Conway sporadic simple groups, the Leech lattice symmetries, surreal numbers, and the Game of Life. Conway loved concrete finite existence questions where every algebraic invariant aligns cleanly yet geometry stubbornly refuses to show its hand — making the 99-graph his signature \$1,000 combinatorial puzzle.
