# Top Mathematicians — The Bateman–Horn Conjecture

_Ten figures who shaped the problem, past and present._

| # | Mathematician | Contribution |
|---|---------------|--------------|
| 1 | Paul T. Bateman | Co-formulated the quantitative asymptotic conjecture and Euler product (1962) |
| 2 | Roger A. Horn | Co-authored the foundational 1962 paper and numerical verification |
| 3 | Viktor Bunyakovsky | Posed the single-polynomial qualitative precursor (1857) |
| 4 | G. H. Hardy & J. E. Littlewood | Formulated the linear $k$-tuple and quadratic prime asymptotics (1923) |
| 5 | Andrzej Schinzel | Formulated qualitative Hypothesis H for polynomial systems (1958) |
| 6 | Atle Selberg | Identified the sieve parity barrier blocking direct combinatorial proofs |
| 7 | Henryk Iwaniec | Proved $n^2+1$ takes infinitely many $P_2$ values (1978) and co-proved $x^2+y^4$ primes (1998) |
| 8 | John Friedlander | Co-proved the $x^2 + y^4$ prime asymptotic theorem (1998) |
| 9 | James Maynard | Proved bounded gaps between primes and primes represented by incomplete norm forms |
| 10 | Will Sawin & Mark Shusterman | Proved function-field analogues of Chowla and Bateman–Horn over $\mathbb{F}_q[t]$ |
