# Originator(s) — The Bateman–Horn Conjecture

_Biography, background, and the ideas that led here._

**Paul T. Bateman (1919–2012)** was an analytic number theorist at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign who worked extensively on additive number theory, modular forms, and prime distribution. **Roger A. Horn (born 1942)** — later famous for *Matrix Analysis* (Horn & Johnson) — was an undergraduate working with Bateman when they derived the general local-to-global heuristic and computed numerical tables testing the Euler product $C(f_1, \dots, f_k)$ in 1962.
