# Attempts — The Bateman–Horn Conjecture

_Notable attempts, near-misses, retracted proofs._

- **Convergence Subtlety of the Euler Product.** Unlike simple linear forms where the Euler product converges absolutely, for general irreducible polynomials $C(f_1, \dots, f_k)$ converges only *conditionally* (relying on the prime ideal theorem / Chebotarev density theorem), making high-precision numerical computation of $C$ itself a delicate analytic task (Conrad; Alekseyev).
- **Two-Variable vs. One-Variable Breakthroughs.** Friedlander–Iwaniec ($x^2 + y^4$) and Maynard (incomplete norm forms) succeeded only because two or more variables supply sufficient bilinear structure to beat the parity barrier; no known specialization descends to single-variable $n^2 + 1$.
