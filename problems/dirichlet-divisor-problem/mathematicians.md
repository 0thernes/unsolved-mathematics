# Top Mathematicians — The Dirichlet Divisor Problem

_Ten figures who shaped the problem, past and present._

| # | Mathematician | Contribution |
|---|---------------|--------------|
| 1 | Peter Gustav Lejeune Dirichlet | Founded the problem and proved $\Delta(x) = O(x^{1/2})$ (1849) |
| 2 | Georgy Voronoi | Proved $\Delta(x) = O(x^{1/3}\log x)$ and established Voronoi summation (1903) |
| 3 | G. H. Hardy | Proved $\theta \ge 1/4$ and the $L^2$ asymptotic (1916) |
| 4 | Johannes van der Corput | Invented exponential-sum differencing to break $\theta < 1/3$ (1922) |
| 5 | Enrico Bombieri & Henryk Iwaniec | Invented the discrete Hardy–Littlewood method for exponential sums (1986) |
| 6 | C. J. Mozzochi | Co-proved $\theta \le 7/22$ with Iwaniec (1988) |
| 7 | Martin N. Huxley | Systematized the discrete Hardy–Littlewood method to $\theta \le 131/416$ (2003) |
| 8 | Jean Bourgain & Nigel Watt | Achieved the current record $\theta \le 517/1648 \approx 0.31371$ (2017) |
| 9 | Kannan Soundararajan | Proved sharp resonance-method $\Omega$-results for $\Delta(x)$ (2003) |
| 10 | Youness Lamzouri & Kamalakshya Mahatab | Refined sharp 2026 $\Omega$-bounds for the divisor and circle problems |
