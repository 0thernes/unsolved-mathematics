# Top Mathematicians — The Dirichlet Divisor Problem

_Ten figures who shaped the problem, past and present._

| # | Name | Era | Role | Contribution |
|---|------|-----|------|--------------|
| 1 | Peter Gustav Lejeune Dirichlet | 1849 | originator | Founded the problem and proved $\Delta(x) = O(x^{1/2})$ (1849). |
| 2 | Georgy Voronoi | 1903 | breakthrough | Proved $\Delta(x) = O(x^{1/3}\log x)$ and established Voronoi summation (1903). |
| 3 | G. H. Hardy | 1916 | breakthrough | Proved $\theta \ge 1/4$ and the $L^2$ asymptotic (1916). |
| 4 | Johannes van der Corput | 1922 | breakthrough | Invented exponential-sum differencing to break $\theta < 1/3$ (1922). |
| 5 | Enrico Bombieri & Henryk Iwaniec | 1986 | breakthrough | Invented the discrete Hardy–Littlewood method for exponential sums (1986). |
| 6 | C. J. Mozzochi | 1988 | breakthrough | Co-proved $\theta \le 7/22$ with Iwaniec (1988). |
| 7 | Martin N. Huxley | 1990s–2003 | breakthrough | Systematized the discrete Hardy–Littlewood method to $\theta \le 131/416$ (2003). |
| 8 | Jean Bourgain & Nigel Watt | 2017 | frontier | Achieved the current record $\theta \le 517/1648 \approx 0.31371$ (2017). |
| 9 | Kannan Soundararajan | 2003–present | breakthrough | Proved sharp resonance-method $\Omega$-results for $\Delta(x)$ (2003). |
| 10 | Youness Lamzouri & Kamalakshya Mahatab | 2020s–present | frontier | Refined sharp 2026 $\Omega$-bounds for the divisor and circle problems. |
