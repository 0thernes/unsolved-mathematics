# Originator(s) — The Dirichlet Divisor Problem

_Biography, background, and the ideas that led here._

**Peter Gustav Lejeune Dirichlet (1805–1859)** founded modern analytic number theory by introducing Dirichlet $L$-series (1837) and the analytic class number formula (1839), and succeeding Gauss at Göttingen in 1855. His 1849 memoir *Über die Bestimmung der mittleren Werthe in der Zahlentheorie* introduced the hyperbola trick to balance $a \le \sqrt{x}$ against $b \le \sqrt{x}$, turning divisor-sum asymptotics into a sharp geometric lattice-point problem.
