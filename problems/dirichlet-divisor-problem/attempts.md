# Attempts — The Dirichlet Divisor Problem

_Notable attempts, near-misses, retracted proofs._

- **Why RH / Lindelöf Is Insufficient.** Many newcomers assume the Riemann Hypothesis settles $\Delta(x) = O(x^{1/4+\varepsilon})$ via Perron's formula applied to $\zeta(s)^2$. However, shifting the contour of $\zeta(s)^2 x^s / s$ to $\operatorname{Re}(s) = 1/2$ encounters the **size of the vertical integral** of $\zeta(1/2+it)^2$, which only bounds $\Delta(x) \ll x^{1/2-\delta}$ unless phase oscillation of $x^{it} \zeta(1/2+it)^2$ is exploited — which brings one straight back to Voronoi's exponential sum.
- **Unrefereed Preprint Claims.** Periodic arXiv/preprint uploads claim $\theta = 1/4$ via elementary rearrangements of the hyperbola sum or unrigorous stationary-phase truncations; all fail to control the tail of Voronoi's expansion uniformly in $x$.
