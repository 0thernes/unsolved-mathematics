## 4. Deductive, Inductive & Abductive Inference Analysis

To analyze both this repository and the broader enterprise of tackling hard unsolved mathematics with AI tools, we separate three classical modes of logical inference:

### 4.1 Deductive Inference (Necessary Consequences from Verified Premises)

1. **Formal Kernel Soundness (Conditional Certainty):**
   - *Premise 1:* Every theorem in `formal/Solution.lean` is accepted by all independent Lean 4.35.0 kernels (`lean`, `leanchecker-paranoid`, `con-leche`, `con-ron`, `nanoda_bin`, plus `lean4lean` in Linux CI) using only axioms $\subseteq \{\text{propext}, \text{Classical.choice}, \text{Quot.sound}\}$.
   - *Premise 2:* `formal/NonVacuity.lean` constructs concrete finite witnesses proving every hypothesis predicate is non-empty, and `formal/Challenge.lean` fixes the exact target types.
   - *Deductive Conclusion:* Conditional on the consistency of Martin-Löf Type Theory + classical choice + quotient soundness and the definitional faithfulness audited in `STATEMENT_FIDELITY.md`, all 14 theorems (including Collatz Positivity Localization $\rho_w \le -1$, the $\varepsilon$-invariance $3n-1$ barrier, Erdős–Straus 834/840 Mordell covering, Lonely Runner sharpness, and Brocard $n \le 12$) hold with **deductive necessity** — zero finite counterexamples can exist.
2. **Barrier Deduction (Why Elementary Collatz Proofs Fail):**
   - *Premise 1 (Machine-checked `collatz_sign_invariant_barrier`):* The affine cycle identity $(2^S - 3^k) n = C(w)$ holds identically for $3n + \varepsilon$ ($\varepsilon = \pm 1$), and $\varepsilon = -1$ admits the positive integer cycle $5 \to 7 \to 5$.
   - *Deductive Conclusion:* Any proof argument that relies solely on properties invariant under $\varepsilon \mapsto -\varepsilon$ (such as pure 2-adic/3-adic valuation counts, word-length parity, or sign-blind modular lifting) **necessarily fails** to prove the Collatz conjecture. This eliminates an entire infinite class of doomed proof attempts by pure deduction.
3. **Invariant Preservation in Software Validation:**
   - *Premise:* If `generate.py --check`, `validate.py`, `build_data_manifest.py --check`, and `tests/mutation_check.py` (100% mutant kill rate) all pass in CI, then zero rank drift, zero duplicate RAG chunk IDs, zero schema violations, and zero unhedged active proof claims exist in the checked commit.

### 4.2 Inductive Inference (Empirical Generalizations from Observed Evidence)

1. **Empirical Hallucination Rate of LLM-Generated Mathematical Citations:**
   - *Observation:* Auditing all 552 identifier-bearing rows drafted by an LLM without live API access yielded **109 invalid identifiers (19.75%)** — 86 wrong-paper IDs (15.58%) and 23 HTTP 404s (4.17%) — even on rows self-labeled `high-confidence`.
   - *Inductive Generalization:* Unchecked LLM citation tables in research mathematics carry an expected ~15–25% identifier corruption rate regardless of self-reported confidence. Therefore, **zero-trust live API verification is mandatory** before any identifier earns a `verified` flag.
2. **Empirical Pattern Across Hard Open Problems (Why They Resist Resolution):**
   - *Observation across 106 dossiers:* Problems that stay open for >50 years almost universally sit at the **collision boundary of two incompatible structures**:
     - Additive vs. multiplicative structure in $\mathbb{Z}$ (Goldbach, Twin Primes, ABC, Collatz, Erdős–Straus, Riemann Hypothesis).
     - Local vs. global rigidity / smoothness (Navier–Stokes blow-up, Yang–Mills mass gap, Smooth 4D Poincaré, Hodge).
     - Combinatorial complexity vs. algebraic/analytic randomness (P vs NP, Unique Games, Hadwiger, Kakeya).
   - *Inductive Generalization:* Breakthroughs (e.g., Wang–Zahl 3D Kakeya 2025, Guth–Maynard 2024, Wiles 1995, Perelman 2003) almost never come from pushing an existing single-structure tool slightly harder; they come from **new bridge invariants** translating between the colliding structures (multiscale graininess / sticky Kakeya, modularity of Galois representations, Ricci flow entropy).
3. **Software Drift Induction:**
   - *Observation:* Every check in `v0.1.0`–`v0.2.0` that was optional, un-exercised by negative tests, or documented without enforcement (`jsonschema` fallback, README rank refresh, single-word hedge allowlist, `tiktoken` fallback) drifted silently within months.
   - *Inductive Rule:* "Unenforced invariants always rot." Every claimed invariant must be backed by a deterministic CI check AND a negative/mutation test proving the check actually fires.

### 4.3 Abductive Inference (Inference to the Best Explanation)

1. **Why did `unsolved-mathematics` accumulate 7 silent bugs, 109 bad identifiers, and a 4.5-month stranded branch despite serious good-faith effort?**

## 5. Philosophy of Mathematics & Scientific Epistemology

This repository's architecture is grounded in five explicit philosophical commitments:

1. **Popperian Falsifiability (`CLAIMS.md`):** Karl Popper argued that scientific claims derive their epistemic force not from how confidently they are stated, but from **what observable event would refute them**. Every claim C-01 through C-13 in [`CLAIMS.md`](../../CLAIMS.md) carries an explicit *"What Would Prove Us Wrong"* column. Unfalsifiable claims ("our CSS ranking is objectively correct") are explicitly banned.
2. **Lakatosian *Proofs and Refutations* (Epistemic Quarantine over Silent Deletion):** Imre Lakatos showed that mathematical progress advances not through spotless monotonic theorem-accumulation, but through *conjectures, counterexamples, lemma-incorporation, and honest refutation*. Rather than quietly hiding failed Collatz approaches, we retain them in `quarantine/` or under explicit **WITHDRAWN / REFUTED** headers (`PROOF-SKETCH-AFFINE-COCYCLE-RIGIDITY-2026.md`) and distill why they failed into machine-checked barrier theorems (`collatz_sign_invariant_barrier`).
3. **Duhem–Quine Holism & Multi-Layer Verification:** Pierre Duhem and W. V. O. Quine demonstrated that no claim is tested in isolation — every test relies on auxiliary hypotheses (compiler correctness, kernel soundness, definition fidelity, citation database accuracy). That is why a single check is never enough: we triangulate across **six independent Lean kernels**, **non-vacuity witnesses**, **independent Python differential oracles**, **mutation testing**, and **live primary-source APIs**.
4. **Hilbert's Formalism vs. Gödel & the Representational-Mismatch Problem:** Formalization in Lean 4 delivers Hilbert's dream of mechanical syntactic certainty — *once a theorem is inside the formal language*. Yet translating informal mathematics into formal types remains a human/philosophical responsibility (`STATEMENT_FIDELITY.md`). A machine-checked proof of a subtly mangled definition is worthless ("formalization theater"). Thus formal kernels **anchor** mathematical truth; they do not replace human mathematical comprehension and community scrutiny.
5. **Social Epistemology & Honest Provenance:** Following Helen Longino's account of scientific objectivity as transformative criticism across diverse epistemic communities, single-author / single-model work — even with automated checks — is **pre-peer-review**. Radical honesty about our `1/4` AI self-review and `0/106` human-referee status is not a weakness; it is the prerequisite for genuine mathematical credibility.
